## Brand & Style
The design system projects authority, engineering rigor, and forward-looking industrial capability tailored for institutional enterprise clients and government giga-projects across Saudi Arabia. The aesthetic pairs high-precision Corporate Modernism with generous architectural whitespace, evoking stability, compliance, and technological competence.

The audience consists of C-level executives, procurement directors, project management consultants (PMCs), and engineering heads. Visual interactions must convey calm assurance, high operational readiness, and spotless execution. The interface avoids frivolous ornamentation in favor of deliberate structure, hairline technical borders, micro-radii, and luminous accents drawn directly from industrial precision engineering.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in institutional trust and environmental stewardship. 

- **Primary (`#0B3C8A`)**: Deep Navy Cobalt provides executive weight. Used for primary CTAs, key brand banners, navigation anchors, and authoritative headers.
- **Secondary (`#43B02A`)**: Vibrant Leaf Green functions as a precision accent. Reserved for verified credentials, sustainability metrics, status indicators, active states, and tactical conversion points. It must be used sparingly to preserve contrast and impact.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`)**: Cyan Spark highlights interactive telemetry, active tabs, subtle focal rings, and secondary metrics.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Slate 900 anchors all body copy and primary typography, preventing harsh true-black fatigue.

### Background Surface Roles
- Canvas default: `#FFFFFF` pure crisp white.
- Section alternations & card containers: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) and `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100).
- Borders & dividers: Hairline `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) ensuring razor-sharp architectural grid alignment.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural balance across English and multilingual engineering documentation. Its tall x-height, clear apertures, and geometric stems maintain high legibility across dense spec sheets, project dashboards, and executive proposal decks.

Headlines employ negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and substantial weight (`700` to `800`) to anchor technical authority. Micro-labels, KPI tags, and status ribbons use uppercase styling with positive letter spacing (`0.05em`) to ensure instant readability in field conditions and high-density tables.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at a maximum width of `1440px`. The spacing scale follows a disciplined 8pt base unit designed to structure large industrial catalogs, multi-disciplinary service breakdowns, and project timelines.

- **Desktop (≥1280px)**: 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (32px), `margin-lg` (64px). Generous 96px–128px vertical padding separates primary enterprise service divisions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` (24px), `margin` (32px). Cards shift to 2-column or 4-column groupings.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (16px), `margin-sm` (16px). Grids collapse to single-column cascades; horizontal metric bars become scrollable or vertically stacked rows.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through structured tonal layering and low-contrast borders rather than exaggerated drop shadows. The goal is an architectural, precision-machined quality.

- **Surface Layer 0 (Canvas)**: `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`.
- **Surface Layer 1 (Card & Module)**: `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px solid border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Hover & Floating Elements)**: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(11, 60, 138, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 60, 138, 0.06)`. The subtle blue tint anchors the shadow into the primary brand palette.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Modals)**: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with an explicit border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with backdrop blur (`blur(12px)`) and a subtle bottom border `#E2E8F0`.

## Shapes
The design uses soft micro-radii (`0.25rem` / `4px` baseline, up to `0.5rem` / `8px` for larger cards and modal containers). This geometric discipline echoes architectural blueprints, steel framing, and industrial infrastructure. 

Pill shapes are strictly reserved for live project status badges (e.g., "Active Site", "ISO Certified") and floating alert chips. Interactive controls such as buttons and input fields adhere strictly to `0.25rem`–`0.375rem` corners to preserve their technical, non-whimsical corporate presence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0B3C8A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px`, padding `12px 24px`. Hover: `#082C66`. Focused: 2px ring offset `#0284C7`.
- **Secondary / Accent**: Background `#43B02A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px`. Reserved for immediate operational CTAs (e.g., "Request RFP", "Emergency Maintenance Dispatch"). Hover: `#379622`.
- **Outline / Technical**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#0B3C8A`, text `#0B3C8A`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` backgrounds, `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders, and `4px` corner radii.
- Hover states feature a subtle top border accent in `#0B3C8A` (2px) alongside a slight lift to Elevation Level 1.
- Header tiles include a dedicated icon compartment tinted with 10% `#0B3C8A` opacity.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, border-radius `4px`, padding `0 14px`.
- Active focus transitions border to `#0B3C8A` with a 3px glow ring in `rgba(11, 60, 138, 0.12)`.
- Error state: Border `#DC2626` with red validation micro-text.

### Badges & Chips
- Status indicators feature muted, high-legibility backgrounds:
  - *Active / Compliant*: Background `#EBF7EB`, border `#B6E6AE`, text `#256B17`.
  - *Institutional / Phase*: Background `#E9F0FA`, border `#BFD6F6`, text `#0B3C8A`.
  - *Operational Pending*: Background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#B45309`.
- Typography: `label-sm` tracking at `0.05em`.

### Lists & Key Metric Displays
- Industrial KPI blocks display values in `display-lg` (`#0B3C8A`) with associated metric descriptors set in uppercase `label-md` (`#64748B`).
- Spec lists feature hairline separators (`1px solid #F1F5F9`) with icon-anchored list items (16px checkmarks in `#43B02A`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base dimension `18px`, radius `3px` (checkbox) or circular (radio), border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`.
- Checked state fills with `#0B3C8A` containing a crisp white tick mark.
